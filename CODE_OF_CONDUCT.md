# Code of Conduct

Our community welcomes contributors regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. This policy applies to project issues, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

Be respectful, explain technical disagreement with evidence, accept corrections, and make space for others. Focus criticism on work and behavior, not personal characteristics. Avoid insults, threats, harassment, unwanted sexual attention, impersonation, and publishing personal or confidential information without consent.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately through the hosting platform's private reporting feature or directly to a maintainer using the contact published on their profile. If no private channel is available, open an issue requesting a confidential contact without identifying people or describing the incident publicly. A public release must provide an operational private contact route.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports when possible, preserve confidentiality, consider evidence, and avoid conflicts of interest. They may request a correction, remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a participant according to severity and repeated behavior. They should explain the decision privately where practical.

An affected person may request reconsideration by a different maintainer when available. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation. Maintainers are accountable to the same policy.

This policy is original project text. The [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) is a useful external resource for community governance.
